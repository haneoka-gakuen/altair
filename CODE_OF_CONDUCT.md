# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and curious. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained personal attacks are not
accepted. Critique work and decisions with evidence; do not attack people.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository
maintainers. They will minimize disclosure, avoid conflicts of interest, and
document enforcement decisions.

This applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
